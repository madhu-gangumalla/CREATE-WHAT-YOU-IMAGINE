A SIX-DAY AI CREATION EXPERIENCE

A proposed classroom initiative for student creativity and practical AI awareness.

Objective

Introduce students to practical applications of Artificial Intelligence through demonstrations and guided creative activities.

Format

  • Duration: Six days (approximately 45–60 minutes per session, subject to college approval).
  • Venue: College classroom or computer laboratory.
  • Participants: Interested students.
  • Method: Live demonstrations and guided hands-on activities.
  • Experience level: Beginner-friendly.
  • Programming knowledge: Not required.

Proposed schedule

  • Day 1, Imagine: Introduction to AI-powered creation, turning ordinary ideas into creative concepts.
  • Day 2, Explore: Experimenting with different AI tools and their practical uses.
  • Day 3, Learn: Exploring how AI can make studying and learning more interactive.
  • Day 4, Express: Creative communication and digital expression.
  • Day 5, Connect: Creating useful solutions for everyday student problems.
  • Day 6, Showcase: Sharing ideas, experiments and simple digital creations.

Expected outcomes

  • Better understanding of practical AI applications.
  • Exposure to AI-assisted digital creation.
  • Development of creative thinking and problem-solving.
  • Confidence to experiment with new technologies.
  • Opportunity to showcase student-created ideas.

Resources required

  • Projector or display screen.
  • Internet access.
  • Smartphones or computers where available.
  • Access to free AI tools.

Request

We request the college’s consideration of this initiative as a possible classroom activity. The format and schedule can be adapted according to the college’s convenience.

Create What You Imagine: 6 Days. 6 Products. This is an independent educational proposal. The themes are a suggested outline and can be adapted, and it has not been endorsed by any institution.
